Engineering ships versus the established small-craft fleet

Compare hull sections,wing profiles,structural divisions,materials and construction detail. Each image is fitted independently: this is a style comparison,not physical game scale. These are existing actual model previews;camera and lighting vary between authoring passes. Scout/research references are labelled separately.

Terran

Partial match: red fields and cool hull colours connect the designs,but the engineering ships have broad box-like bodies while the small fleet uses wedges,layered plates and round engine details.

Rigellian

Partial match: low rounded shells,gold caps and copper engines agree. The engineering ships are silver-dominant with green strips;the established small fleet is green-dominant. Material-region balance needs review.

Braccatian

Partial match: coral,ochre and brass belong together,but the engineering barrels and broad black reinforcement strips read more industrial than the tapered,lobed small craft.

Mikotaj

Canonical-style pass:three reviewed cycles introduced slate-blue/gunmetal structure,dulled-gold accents,tapered rail profiles and slimmer faceted pods related to Ylikt-shan and Hiero. Gold and primary paint map medians match the canonical authored palette. Utility frames and tools remain functionally distinct;user artistic acceptance is pending.

Artemian

Partial match: silver and green remain recognisable. Smooth oval engineering shells lose the wedge-shaped green base and separate silver fairings of the small fleet;material distribution and section breaks need review.

Mauna

Close structural match: rectangular volumes,stepped sections,slots and grey/blue-grey materials connect the ships. Surface finish differs;lighting and separate rendering passes limit exact colour judgement.

Achaean

Close match: copper spindle hulls,thin red/silver crescents and sparse hatches repeat the fleet language. The repaired ship now has mirrored wings;small functional tooling remains distinct.
