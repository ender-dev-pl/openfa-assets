Engineering ships versus the established small-craft fleet

Compare hull sections,wing profiles,structural divisions,materials and construction detail. Each image is fitted independently: this is a style comparison,not physical game scale. Canonical GLB references use the same review studio where available;other previews retain their existing cameras and lighting. Scout/research references are labelled separately.

Terran

Canonical-style pass:two reviewed cycles introduced a tapered layered deck,swept faceted gondolas,quiet red fields and canonical violet-grey/brass PBR targets. Actual complete Hull GLBs are compared in the same studio with construction layers omitted. Terraform retains its rotor;repair has six channels including independently closing jaws. Utility bodies remain broader than the combat wedge;user artistic acceptance is pending.

Rigellian

Canonical-style pass:one reviewed cycle introduced a green-dominant elongated oval hull,gold fields/crown,slate gondolas and darker ventral casings. Palette/effective PBR values derive from current canonical GLBs;matching-studio references are available. Repair has six animation channels including separate jaws. User artistic acceptance is pending.

Braccatian

Partial match: coral,ochre and brass belong together,but the engineering barrels and broad black reinforcement strips read more industrial than the tapered,lobed small craft.

Mikotaj

Canonical-style pass:three reviewed cycles introduced slate-blue/gunmetal structure,dulled-gold accents,tapered rail profiles and slimmer faceted pods related to Ylikt-shan and Hiero. Gold and primary paint map medians match the canonical authored palette. Utility frames and tools remain functionally distinct;user artistic acceptance is pending.

Artemian

Canonical-style pass:two reviewed cycles introduced a pointed green deck,separated faceted silver fairings and canonical material medians. Current canonical GLB references are rendered in the same review studio. Repair has independent sliders,wrist and closing jaws;user artistic acceptance is pending.

Mauna

Close structural match: rectangular volumes,stepped sections,slots and grey/blue-grey materials connect the ships. Surface finish differs;lighting and separate rendering passes limit exact colour judgement.

Achaean

Close match: copper spindle hulls,thin red/silver crescents and sparse hatches repeat the fleet language. The repaired ship now has mirrored wings;small functional tooling remains distinct.
