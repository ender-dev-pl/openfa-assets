# Fleet Battleship retrospective concept and GLB feedback

Programme openfa-assets #248. Latest SciTek canonical Blend/GLB remains unchanged. This records a missing retrospective concept/render stage; it does not certify final fidelity or introduce a replacement payload.

One built-in ImageGen request used original PS06.VID frames 0/15/30/45. The exact prompt, reference identities, selected image and limits are in [the review](fleet-battleship-protocol-review.json). The principal arched transverse wing, long central forebody, red upper markings and single large bronze aft/underside engine are preserved. Small routing, attachment and endcap details in the concept are interpretations. Original pixels remain private.

Fresh Blender 4.5.15 renders reopen native and independently import GLB at frame 1. Both sides compare evaluated geometry, use the same camera/light rig and fit complete-object views with clear alpha margins. The report binds the wrapper and unchanged shared renderer, model bytes and four actual images. Two views are scoped appearance/geometry evidence, not exhaustive topology, UV, animation or client calibration acceptance.

Feedback: the GLB has a simpler flatter wing chord/shoulder outline and a narrower red crown stripe than the broad flowing original markings; its slender central body is more cylindrical than the more faceted original/concept forebody. Current material/detail contrast is simpler and smoother than the concept. These limits are explicit while preserving the user's canonical source decision. Concept additions do not become legacy facts.

Interactive and Markdown galleries include the concept and actual imported front/rear views. Gameplay remains the documented frame-1 pose with presentation rotation disabled. Mounts, size, complete animation handling and final client/user acceptance remain separate. No duplicate gameplay/menu model or new geometry is introduced.
