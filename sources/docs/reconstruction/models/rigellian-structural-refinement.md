# Rigellian visible structural refinement

Tracked in [#185](https://github.com/ender-dev-pl/openfa-assets/issues/185).
The user clarified that refining means missing visible details and increased
overall fidelity/detail, not imperceptible microgeometry. Complete Colony
Database culture evidence must guide materials, form and technology choices.

## Cultural evidence

Read all nine descriptive sections of the Rigellian CDB entry, records 4–32,
and the additional cross-culture mentions. `rigellian-lore-evidence.json` records
the original source SHA, exact offsets/lengths and individual record hashes,
paraphrased findings and explicit authoring decisions. Architecture describes
elegant, ergonomic facilities even for mining, comfortable by Terran standards
without Artemian luxury. Research is normal colony life, not merely a military
activity. The entry does not name a mandatory construction alloy. Bronze and
green appearances are grounded in the actual paletted graphics; identifying a
specific metal or the function of an ambiguous coloured detail remains inferred.

No applicable Rigellian-specific extractor Sci-Tek blueprint binding was found
in the presentation catalogue. The located mine research information bindings
are ST00 (Mk2 Mine) and ST01 (Mk2 Deep Bore Mine). Those Terran research subjects
do not establish RI20's exterior. RI20's own surface body, readable catalogue
phase and highlighted phase 40 guide this refinement. The original ENGLISH.TXT
function describes a universal ore extractor, not an external drill animation.

## RI20 Extractor

The prior 17-mesh study read as a largely smooth capsule, hiding most of its
small lower drum. Its first authoring documentation explicitly deferred this
artistic pass. The new study restores features visible at ordinary gallery size:

- a broader stepped lower casing and narrower rounded head;
- a pronounced attached side equipment pod;
- three exposed broad horizontal mechanism ribs and four upper drum contacts;
- a segmented green lower fitting with retaining sides;
- three readable gold front fittings, the side fittings and upper inspection face;
- two actual recessed front slots with rooted backings and body-following rims.

The lower-to-upper casing radius ratio is measured from actual surface rays at
1.31. Lower ribs extend about 0.055 beyond the mechanism core. The front slots
are actual cavities with about 0.084 visible depth to their backing faces;
backings intersect their casing roots rather than floating inside the recesses.
These are checks on visible construction, not micro-detail scores.

The bronze/green finish uses independently authored colour regions and existing
authored source wear/roughness, packed into the Blend and GLB. Green marks are
kept as surface appearance; the source does not prove an external pipe network.
Their exact material and hidden mechanisms remain unresolved. There is no
invented drill, operating cycle or exhaust. The casing and assembled details
are newly authored; earlier studies and their files are unchanged.

Editable sources and metadata are in
`sources/docs/assets/reconstruction/rigellian-refined/ri20/`. Whole, rear, front,
side, top and mechanism views, independent imported views and a source-bound
validation report are in the corresponding `gallery/media/` directory. A new
packed colour map lives under `sources/materials/reconstruction/rigellian-refined/ri20/`.

Native Blender reopen and standalone GLB import verify all 39 mesh objects,
positive closed welded surfaces, identity placement/hierarchy, actual shoulder
ratio, rib geometry, slot depth and attachment, visible side silhouette, green
segments and front/drum fittings. Native/imported positions and named material
bindings are compared, and three actual paired whole/detail renders check
framing, silhouette and colour. Inspect the validation JSON for exact render
hashes and measured errors. Technical validity does not establish final artistic
acceptance or calibrated gameplay placement.

The full Rigellian pass remains active. This document records a structural
milestone, not completion of all 35 models or all review-ledger gates.
