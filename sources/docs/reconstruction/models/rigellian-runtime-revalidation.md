# Existing Rigellian building revalidation (#1106)

All 23 currently selected Rigellian building sources (825 asset meshes) passed their existing source-specific native/import verifiers in actual Blender 4.5.15 LTS. Relates to #1099, #948 and #946. No generator was executed and no existing model/refinement was recreated.

`revalidate_rigellian_buildings.py` derives selection from the inventory and validates each selected Blend hash. It locates the first ten historical verifier modules by explicit names with resolved target-root checks; the later thirteen are mapped by the SHA-256 recorded in their technical report. Every source directory is copied byte-for-byte to a unique ignored working directory. Matching root globals are redirected to that copy, leaving assertion bodies and tolerances unchanged. New technical reports and renders are produced there, preserving original accepted evidence. Every original file is hashed again after its verifier returns. All 23 returned successfully; the wrapper completed with final success marker and exit zero, with no caught failures.

Checks differ by model. Existing assertions cover topology/materials/bounds/placement, supported assemblies and real recesses/openings, sampled physical contact graphs and fittings, measured dents and authored image/UV checks where implemented. Ground Deck additionally checked native/imported transforms and triangle intersections at 61 sampled poses, the unobstructed deep shaft with nine rays, wall-light visibility and actual closed/intermediate/open renders. These tests do not prove continuous swept-volume clearance or exact recovered mechanism/timing.

New reports and complete recorded process output are under `docs/assets/reconstruction/evidence/rigellian-runtime-revalidation/`. `summary.json` binds original files, exact verifier identities, copied technical reports and actual pass/fail outcomes. `review_rigellian_revalidation.py` verified current originals/scripts/reports and nonempty working-copy native/import overview pixels, then produced two actual overview sheets. Both were inspected. The overview reviewer does not calculate quantitative pixel parity or accept every state/detail; full generated working renders remain in the per-session ignored directory identified in the summary.

The inventory verifier also passed for 303 definitions / 1444 reference occurrences while revalidation was running, confirming earlier accepted evidence was intact. All original per-file hashes were checked unchanged again by the overview reviewer after completion. Source files, original reports/render evidence and ledger gates were not updated. No wear retrofit, artwork refining or scaffolding work occurred.
