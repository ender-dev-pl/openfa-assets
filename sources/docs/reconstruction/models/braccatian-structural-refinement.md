This records verified individual milestones in the active all-38 pass. Final

## BR22 Pithoi

The original surface parts and all 41 catalogue reveal/highlight phases were
inspected. They show a gold lower cylinder, two broad rose shoulder regions,
an axial gold neck/globe, large circular face fitting, dark middle fields and
a lower pale equipment column. The full functional narrative explicitly names
decorated storage jars. Full Braccatian CDB guidance supports substantial,
deliberately decorated architecture, but does not establish a universal alloy
or arbitrary clan heraldry.

The generic organic placeholder is replaced in a separate study by a profiled
double-shoulder jar, ribbed gold lower cylinder, actual annular port rims with
exposed pale-blue faces, large conformal gold face fitting, dark middle fields,
red neck inset and terminal globe. The old trident and generic small square
panels are not copied. Exact profiles, dimensions, eleven flutes, fitting depths
and hidden rear construction remain authored. Dark fields are surface equipment
insets, not claims of recovered ventilation or storage mechanisms.

All thirty-six new meshes receive independently authored colour/roughness maps
with grime, stains and scratches; the main castings also have shallow physical
dents. Legacy pixels are not texture inputs. This is an entirely new assembly;
no unchanged-geometry claim is made for old placeholder components. Its original
Blend/GLB remain separate and selectable.

Native Blender reopen and independent GLB tests check closed positive consistently
wound geometry, marked complete collection, placement/2x2 footprint, actual two
shoulder cross-sections/waist, ground/silhouette bounds, fifty-seven sampled part
contacts, exposed port centres, UV/material hierarchy and packed authored maps.
Effective roughness channels are compared correctly through glTF's standard
green-channel roughness transport packing. All eight effective colour/roughness
map arrays match. There is no source-supported operating clip to add.

Three actual matched-camera whole/front/detail render pairs were inspected
against the source. All thresholded silhouettes have IoU 1.0; maximum position
difference is below 1.014e-6 and maximum foreground RGB mean difference below
0.0021/255. Render comparisons do not establish final artistic acceptance or
recovered camera/scale calibration.

## BR04 Source, BR14 Anchor and BR15 Heart-of-the-Sun

For each building, the original surface parts and all 41 catalogue
reveal/highlight phases were inspected. Their separate silhouettes and face
equipment qualify the shared cultural design language; one generic organic
tower cannot represent all three. New assemblies are separate studies and do
not claim geometric retention of the earlier placeholders.

Source now has a slender lower gold cylinder with cast ribs/service rails,
rounded upper vessel, narrow connecting neck, broad rose bands, lower circular
fitting, pale equipment faces, blue collar and a single gold terminal. The old
trident and oversized generic foot are omitted. Its nutrition/ceremonial role
does not establish an exposed ritual mechanism or clan symbol.

Anchor now has a compact ribbed gold cylinder, two broad rose shoulders,
rounded closed dome, large circular upper fitting, middle dark fields and a
lower red/pale service column. It has no separate mast or terminal globe.
Rendered review caught a flat cap left by omitting the old neck; the authored
profile was corrected to a rounded dome. The gravity-nullifier function does
not establish exposed internals or an operating effect.

Heart-of-the-Sun now has two rounded rose chambers joined by a narrow gold
waist, a broad upper face grille, lower circular fitting, separate front upright
fittings, blue collar and a stepped single terminal. The placeholder's side
pods and trident are omitted. The functional text explicitly states fusion
power, but does not describe a visible reactor, animated core or operating loop.

The shared helper infrastructure does not force a common shape: the profiles,
face equipment, material regions and side/front assemblies are case-specific.
Exact profiles, fitting depths, repeated casing counts, hidden surfaces and
alloy identities remain authored. Independent colour/roughness wear and shallow
main-casting dents preserve readable broad construction. No original pixels are
texture inputs and no unsupported idle animation is introduced.

Actual native reopen and independent GLB validation passed for all three:
28 Source meshes, 30 Anchor meshes and 20 Heart-of-the-Sun meshes. Closed
positive consistently wound geometry, complete marked collections, original
2x2 footprints, root/hierarchy and packed effective colour/roughness arrays are
checked in both formats. Source has 41 sampled part contacts, Anchor 51 and
Heart-of-the-Sun 19; every new part participates in an actual contact test.
Radial ray tests check Source's slender cylinder/narrow neck/upper vessel,
Anchor's two shoulders/waist and rounded dome, and the plant's two chambers.
Anchor's actual port centres remain exposed through annular rims.

All nine whole/front/equipment-detail native/import render pairs have silhouette
IoU 1.0. Maximum position difference is below 1.391e-6 and maximum foreground
RGB mean difference below 0.0019/255. Current paired views and source/before/after
comparisons were inspected. Equipment details intentionally crop terminals;
whole views separately check complete framing. Maps and physical dents are
independently authored, not copies of legacy pixels. Exact hidden construction,
material identity and final artistic acceptance remain open.

New source folders are `sources/docs/assets/reconstruction/braccatian-refined/br04`,
`br14` and `br15`; corresponding actual render/validation reports are under
`gallery/media/docs/assets/reconstruction/braccatian-refined/`. Earlier sources
remain unchanged and selectable. These are three static-body refinements in
the active full-38 programme, not completion of the full race.

## BR03 Radiation Shield, BR13 Eyes-and-Ears and BR16 Aegis

All surface parts and all 41 catalogue phases were inspected for each case.
The full-width BR03 phase sheet preserves all three units; the earlier narrow
crop was insufficient for that review. The user's clarification establishes
three handbell-shaped units with handles. BR03 therefore uses three continuous
outward-flaring gold casings with slender banded handles, narrow rose hem trim,
red front fields and pale side fittings. Separate globular heads/discs and the
old rounded-cylinder placeholders do not represent this construction. No
acoustic clapper, exposed internals or ringing animation is inferred from the
bell-like shape: CDB describes radiation protection for carbon-based life.

BR13 has a tall tapered rose body, gold capsule, blue collar and single mast,
plus a shorter gold cylinder/orb with a blue cap and a visible angular link.
Front pale equipment faces and lower service fields replace the old generic
mast cluster. The lore describes efficient surveillance that is socially
downplayed; the visible link's exact function remains unknown.

BR16 has a broad rose domed foot, gold column with red vertical fields, lobed
gold orb, broad foot fitting and two straight upright side masts joined by low
crossarms. The Aegis screen protects key clan buildings and is described as
weaker than its Terran counterpart. This does not establish an exposed screen
animation. The old kite-shaped loops are absent; the space alongside the orb
is open. Repeated lobe count and exact profiles are authored interpretations.

All three use independently authored colour/roughness wear, shallow main-shell
dents and positive closed geometry. The original models remain separately
selectable and unchanged. Hidden surfaces, exact alloys and dimensional
calibration remain uncertain. The earlier BR03 internal prototype was replaced
before gallery registration in response to the handbell clarification.

Actual native reopen and independent GLB import passed: BR03 has 24 meshes and
21 sampled contacts; BR13 has 25/24 and BR16 22/21. Every part participates in
contact checks. Tests measure BR03's outward-flaring radial sections, single
continuous casing and grounded independent units; BR13's two bodies/link and
BR16's straight masts/open side clearance. Connectivity diagnostics weld only
coincident UV/normal export seams before counting physical components.
Native and imported sources both retain closed positive consistently wound
geometry, original catalogue footprints, full root grouping and effective
packed colour/roughness arrays. Maximum position difference is 1.014e-6.
Nine native/imported whole/front/detail pairs have silhouette IoU 1.0 and
maximum foreground RGB mean difference below 0.0031/255; actual paired renders
were inspected. These are three more studies in the ongoing full-race pass.

## BR06 Longhouse: joined dwellings and open entrances

The original 36x46 paletted building body, separate 46x29 shadow and all 41
catalogue phases were inspected. The film is a fixed-view reveal/highlight
presentation; it does not establish operating or spatial turntable motion.
The functional narrative (English text record 929, offset 72990, 167 bytes)
identifies numerous extended-family living quarters. Broad cultural decoration
therefore does not justify invented ceremonial machinery or clan emblems.

The earlier three isolated generic cones omitted the entire lower complex.
This study restores three joined gold chambers with broad rose terraces,
three extruded rose arched entrances, substantial gold jamb fittings and side
face divisions. Three descending square terraced towers rise from that joined
assembly. Their broad gold/rose planes, stepped shoulders, red upper collars,
single gold tips and visible front fittings follow the legacy silhouette and
colour regions. The entrance openings are actual empty geometry with recessed
space behind them, rather than dark patches applied to solid boxes.

The exact depths, hidden rear lintel, tower sections, division/fitting counts
and arrangement on the original 3x3 footprint remain authored. The source does
not establish a universal bronze alloy or opening entrance animation. Wear is
independently authored in colour/roughness maps; shallow front-casting dents
preserve the tower silhouette. The earlier source remains unchanged/selectable.

Actual Blender reopen and independent GLB import passed for all 61 closed,
positive, consistently wound meshes. A geometric intersection graph with 113
sampled contacts confirms every part belongs to the connected hall assembly.
Ray tests confirm all three entrances remain clear for 0.56 units from the
front test origin at three heights, while separate rays hit the jambs/crowns.
Independent radial sections require the broad-foot tower taper; red collar
materials, original footprint, root hierarchy, complete marked collection and
eight effective colour/roughness map arrays are checked in both formats.
Three paired whole/front/entrance-detail renders have silhouette IoU 1.0,
zero measured position drift and maximum foreground RGB mean difference below
0.0029/255. Native/imported images and source/before/after comparison were
inspected. Entrance-detail deliberately crops upper towers; whole views check
full framing. No operating clip is claimed. Full-race completion remains open.

## BR07 Sports Hall, BR09 Glasshouse and BR10 TechMech

All 41 catalogue phases and both surface descriptors (body and shadow) were
inspected for each case, with enlarged correctly paletted body pixels. Original
functional payloads were read and their exact byte ranges/hashes checked
against the existing functional index. No film establishes operating motion.

Sports Hall is described as a ceremonial gym for communal exercise and male
bonding. Its broad domed body, four front face wells, upper fitting/terminal,
two substantial front pillars with gold globe heads and red/pale lower fields
replace the placeholder's central tower and four generic satellite pods. A low
rear support and connecting front lintel join the assembly; the central lower
entrance interval remains empty. Broad gold divisions follow the dome rather
than introducing invented clan emblems. Render review removed gold strips that
crossed and obscured the face wells. Hidden support construction and exact
relief/placement remain authored; the narrative does not establish a visible
ritual mechanism or animation.

Glasshouse is the security-block counterpart, but its honour-culture narrative
explicitly says security is practically non-existent. The source's slotted
upper cylinder is rebuilt as separate rose uprights between annular castings,
with an inner gold cylinder, rounded rose cap and small tip. This is an
interpretation of visible structural slots, not invented prison machinery.
Three grounded tapering pyramidal supports, lower neck fields and blue/gold
face fittings replace the unsupported generic two-cylinder placeholder. Eight
uprights and the exact internal depth/hidden rear support are authored counts
and dimensions, not quantities established by the tiny sprite.

TechMech is explicitly an extensive and severely practical ship construction
yard, with some heavy-metal-type decoration. It now has a layered cylindrical
body, gold shoulder bands, two side pyramidal modules, three real rising access
steps, three front gold fittings/red fields, waist uprights, upper service faces,
louvers and a circular red fitting. The previous ringed cylinder omitted those
large lower assemblies. Render review separated blue inserts from dark backing
and annular rims to avoid coplanar faces, and placed the waist uprights outside
the upper shell while seating their roots in the broader lower shoulder.
Hidden shipyard internals, exact repeated counts and material identity remain
unknown. No exposed ship assembly process or operating loop is invented.

Each study is a separate complete editable assembly with original catalogue
footprint. Gold/rose/pale/coloured regions are based on the paletted source;
exact alloys remain unknown. Independently authored colour/roughness maps,
shallow main-shell dents and readable wear are included. Prior source files
remain unchanged and selectable; none of their geometry is claimed as retained
inside these new assemblies.

Actual native Blender reopen and independent GLB import passed: 37 Sports Hall
meshes / 62 geometric contacts, 29 Glasshouse meshes / 49 contacts and 37 TechMech
meshes / 76 contacts. All are closed, positive, consistently wound solids; an
intersection graph connects every part in each assembly. Marked collection
membership, root/catalogue footprints, hierarchy and ten effective packed
colour/roughness arrays per case are checked in both formats. Verification
caught and corrected helper-created meshes outside the marked collection;
complete sources were regenerated and independently verified after the fix.

Sports Hall tests measure its broad domed sections, two front pillars/globes,
clear entrance interval and four unobscured face-well centres. Glasshouse tests
cast rays through eight actual upper slots and into a real upright, measure the
three grounded tapered supports and require visible blue fitting centres.
TechMech tests measure the three rising/receding access steps, side modules,
front fittings and unobscured blue/red faces. These geometry checks supplement
counts; counts alone are not evidence of fidelity.

All nine native/imported whole/front/equipment pairs have silhouette IoU 1.0.
Maximum position difference is below 1.392e-6 and maximum foreground RGB mean
difference below 0.0026/255. Current paired views and paletted source/before/after
comparisons were inspected. Details frame the body/equipment rather than the
long TechMech terminal; whole views check complete framing. No new operating
clips are claimed. These three studies bring the current full-race programme
to eleven refined definitions; the remaining 27 decisions remain required.

## BR01 First Step, BR02 Clan Heart and BR05 Aesir's Hall

The full CDB Braccatian records 33-61 were reread with exact byte/hash checks,
including the complete architecture, physical/societal and religious context.
Functional payloads for First Step (record 924, offset 72156), Clan Heart (925,
72273) and Aesir's Hall (928, 72754) were read and checked against the mapped
hashes. The source describes a CPU counterpart, the command centre/Council of
Elders seat with high security, and an air-circulation building explicitly
without ceremonial aspect. Display-oriented architecture does not establish
new clan emblems, anatomical motifs, universal bronze alloy or operating effects.

All 41 paletted catalogue phases and body/shadow layers were inspected for each
case. First Step has four joined broad-foot pyramids, gold/rose planar regions,
blue upper collars/single gold tips, front equipment fields and two bright
triangular portals. The new source restores those joined square-sided bodies
rather than four separate generic cones. The portals have actual shallow
Boolean-cut recesses, dark backing, raised gold frames and pale grille divisions.
Their construction/depth is authored interpretation of the source-visible
bright entrance regions, not a recovered interior or opening animation.

Clan Heart has a tall cylindrical core with shoulder bands and pale vertical
faces, a steep square roof, open gold upper crown and three distinct peripheral
pyramids with blue collars and side face fittings. This recovers the source's
vertical core/roof distinction instead of a single central cone. The high
security narrative does not justify adding weapons or new defensive machinery.
Attachment verification caught an actual gap between the peripheral pyramid
tips and blue collars; their seating was corrected and all sources regenerated
before final acceptance checks.

Aesir's Hall has a low stepped cylindrical body with rose collars/service fields,
a narrow rose neck, broad-foot upper spire, open gold crown and three low
satellites. Red/blue face fittings and plain gold satellite tips follow the
source; unsupported blue upper collars are absent. The visible ornate form is
retained without converting the air system into a ceremonial device. The new
assembly restores the stepped neck and lower construction omitted by the old
four-cone placeholder.

The crown is interpreted as two closed, thick loop castings around a single
mast with a rose seat, leaving actual open intervals. Exact loop counts/depths,
profiles, repeated fitting counts, hidden rear construction and alloys remain
authored. Prior studies remain unchanged/selectable. New wear uses independent
colour/roughness maps and shallow main-casting dents. No original pixels are
texture inputs and no unsupported operating clips are introduced.

Actual Blender reopen and independent GLB import passed for 74 First Step
meshes, 47 Clan Heart meshes and 37 Aesir's Hall meshes. Every mesh is closed,
positive and consistently wound; geometric intersection graphs connect all
parts through 168, 105 and 87 sampled contacts respectively. Complete marked
collections, original 3x3/3x3/2x2 footprints, root hierarchy and ten effective
packed colour/roughness arrays per case are checked in both formats.

First Step tests measure four grounded tapering pyramid sections, four blue
collars, unobscured portal grilles and actual recess depths (~0.114/0.115 units).
Clan Heart tests measure the constant-radius central cylinder (~0.29 units at
two heights), three peripheral pyramids/collars and exposed upper pale faces.
Aesir's Hall tests require the distinct narrow neck (~0.105) and broad upper
spire foot (~0.282), three low satellites and exposed red/blue fittings. Crown
rays pass through actual off-axis openings while separate rays hit the loop
castings. Counts alone are not treated as evidence of reconstruction fidelity.

All eleven current native/imported whole/front/equipment/crown pairs have
silhouette IoU 1.0; maximum position difference is below 1.014e-6 and foreground
RGB mean difference below 0.0032/255. Paired views and source/before/after
comparisons were inspected. Equipment-detail views intentionally crop crowns;
whole views check complete framing and crown-detail views inspect the openings.
These are three additional studies in the full 38-model programme. Fourteen
new refined definitions are now covered; 24 remaining decisions and the final
all-model preservation/animation/gallery audit remain required.
