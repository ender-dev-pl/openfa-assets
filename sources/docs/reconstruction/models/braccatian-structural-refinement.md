# Braccatian visible structural refinements (#190)

This records verified individual milestones in the active all-38 pass. Final
artistic acceptance, hidden construction and gameplay calibration remain open.
Earlier studies are preserved. Original reference pixels remain private.

## BR22 Pithoi

The original surface parts and all 41 catalogue reveal/highlight phases were
inspected. They show a gold lower cylinder, two broad rose shoulder regions,
an axial gold neck/globe, large circular face fitting, dark middle fields and
a lower pale equipment column. The full functional narrative explicitly names
decorated storage jars. Full Braccatian CDB guidance supports substantial,
deliberately decorated architecture, but does not establish a universal alloy
or arbitrary clan heraldry.

The generic organic placeholder is replaced in a separate study by a profiled
double-shoulder jar, ribbed gold lower cylinder, actual annular port rims with
exposed pale-blue faces, large conformal gold face fitting, dark middle fields,
red neck inset and terminal globe. The old trident and generic small square
panels are not copied. Exact profiles, dimensions, eleven flutes, fitting depths
and hidden rear construction remain authored. Dark fields are surface equipment
insets, not claims of recovered ventilation or storage mechanisms.

All thirty-six new meshes receive independently authored colour/roughness maps
with grime, stains and scratches; the main castings also have shallow physical
dents. Legacy pixels are not texture inputs. This is an entirely new assembly;
no unchanged-geometry claim is made for old placeholder components. Its original
Blend/GLB remain separate and selectable.

Native Blender reopen and independent GLB tests check closed positive consistently
wound geometry, marked complete collection, placement/2x2 footprint, actual two
shoulder cross-sections/waist, ground/silhouette bounds, fifty-seven sampled part
contacts, exposed port centres, UV/material hierarchy and packed authored maps.
Effective roughness channels are compared correctly through glTF's standard
green-channel roughness transport packing. All eight effective colour/roughness
map arrays match. There is no source-supported operating clip to add.

Three actual matched-camera whole/front/detail render pairs were inspected
against the source. All thresholded silhouettes have IoU 1.0; maximum position
difference is below 1.014e-6 and maximum foreground RGB mean difference below
0.0021/255. Render comparisons do not establish final artistic acceptance or
recovered camera/scale calibration.

## BR04 Source, BR14 Anchor and BR15 Heart-of-the-Sun

For each building, the original surface parts and all 41 catalogue
reveal/highlight phases were inspected. Their separate silhouettes and face
equipment qualify the shared cultural design language; one generic organic
tower cannot represent all three. New assemblies are separate studies and do
not claim geometric retention of the earlier placeholders.

Source now has a slender lower gold cylinder with cast ribs/service rails,
rounded upper vessel, narrow connecting neck, broad rose bands, lower circular
fitting, pale equipment faces, blue collar and a single gold terminal. The old
trident and oversized generic foot are omitted. Its nutrition/ceremonial role
does not establish an exposed ritual mechanism or clan symbol.

Anchor now has a compact ribbed gold cylinder, two broad rose shoulders,
rounded closed dome, large circular upper fitting, middle dark fields and a
lower red/pale service column. It has no separate mast or terminal globe.
Rendered review caught a flat cap left by omitting the old neck; the authored
profile was corrected to a rounded dome. The gravity-nullifier function does
not establish exposed internals or an operating effect.

Heart-of-the-Sun now has two rounded rose chambers joined by a narrow gold
waist, a broad upper face grille, lower circular fitting, separate front upright
fittings, blue collar and a stepped single terminal. The placeholder's side
pods and trident are omitted. The functional text explicitly states fusion
power, but does not describe a visible reactor, animated core or operating loop.

The shared helper infrastructure does not force a common shape: the profiles,
face equipment, material regions and side/front assemblies are case-specific.
Exact profiles, fitting depths, repeated casing counts, hidden surfaces and
alloy identities remain authored. Independent colour/roughness wear and shallow
main-casting dents preserve readable broad construction. No original pixels are
texture inputs and no unsupported idle animation is introduced.

Actual native reopen and independent GLB validation passed for all three:
28 Source meshes, 30 Anchor meshes and 20 Heart-of-the-Sun meshes. Closed
positive consistently wound geometry, complete marked collections, original
2x2 footprints, root/hierarchy and packed effective colour/roughness arrays are
checked in both formats. Source has 41 sampled part contacts, Anchor 51 and
Heart-of-the-Sun 19; every new part participates in an actual contact test.
Radial ray tests check Source's slender cylinder/narrow neck/upper vessel,
Anchor's two shoulders/waist and rounded dome, and the plant's two chambers.
Anchor's actual port centres remain exposed through annular rims.

All nine whole/front/equipment-detail native/import render pairs have silhouette
IoU 1.0. Maximum position difference is below 1.391e-6 and maximum foreground
RGB mean difference below 0.0019/255. Current paired views and source/before/after
comparisons were inspected. Equipment details intentionally crop terminals;
whole views separately check complete framing. Maps and physical dents are
independently authored, not copies of legacy pixels. Exact hidden construction,
material identity and final artistic acceptance remain open.

New source folders are `sources/docs/assets/reconstruction/braccatian-refined/br04`,
`br14` and `br15`; corresponding actual render/validation reports are under
`gallery/media/docs/assets/reconstruction/braccatian-refined/`. Earlier sources
remain unchanged and selectable. These are three static-body refinements in
the active full-38 programme, not completion of the full race.

## BR03 Radiation Shield, BR13 Eyes-and-Ears and BR16 Aegis

All surface parts and all 41 catalogue phases were inspected for each case.
The full-width BR03 phase sheet preserves all three units; the earlier narrow
crop was insufficient for that review. The user's clarification establishes
three handbell-shaped units with handles. BR03 therefore uses three continuous
outward-flaring gold casings with slender banded handles, narrow rose hem trim,
red front fields and pale side fittings. Separate globular heads/discs and the
old rounded-cylinder placeholders do not represent this construction. No
acoustic clapper, exposed internals or ringing animation is inferred from the
bell-like shape: CDB describes radiation protection for carbon-based life.

BR13 has a tall tapered rose body, gold capsule, blue collar and single mast,
plus a shorter gold cylinder/orb with a blue cap and a visible angular link.
Front pale equipment faces and lower service fields replace the old generic
mast cluster. The lore describes efficient surveillance that is socially
downplayed; the visible link's exact function remains unknown.

BR16 has a broad rose domed foot, gold column with red vertical fields, lobed
gold orb, broad foot fitting and two straight upright side masts joined by low
crossarms. The Aegis screen protects key clan buildings and is described as
weaker than its Terran counterpart. This does not establish an exposed screen
animation. The old kite-shaped loops are absent; the space alongside the orb
is open. Repeated lobe count and exact profiles are authored interpretations.

All three use independently authored colour/roughness wear, shallow main-shell
dents and positive closed geometry. The original models remain separately
selectable and unchanged. Hidden surfaces, exact alloys and dimensional
calibration remain uncertain. The earlier BR03 internal prototype was replaced
before gallery registration in response to the handbell clarification.

Actual native reopen and independent GLB import passed: BR03 has 24 meshes and
21 sampled contacts; BR13 has 25/24 and BR16 22/21. Every part participates in
contact checks. Tests measure BR03's outward-flaring radial sections, single
continuous casing and grounded independent units; BR13's two bodies/link and
BR16's straight masts/open side clearance. Connectivity diagnostics weld only
coincident UV/normal export seams before counting physical components.
Native and imported sources both retain closed positive consistently wound
geometry, original catalogue footprints, full root grouping and effective
packed colour/roughness arrays. Maximum position difference is 1.014e-6.
Nine native/imported whole/front/detail pairs have silhouette IoU 1.0 and
maximum foreground RGB mean difference below 0.0031/255; actual paired renders
were inspected. These are three more studies in the ongoing full-race pass.
