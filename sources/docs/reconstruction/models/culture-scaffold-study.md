# Culture-specific construction scaffolds (#188)

This extends the preserved RIS2 refinement from #186 to every other culture/footprint family used by the 177-building catalogue. There are 18 new families and 264 new states; together with existing RIS2 the set covers 19 used families and 281 states. Nine installed sprite families have no current building dependencies and are not represented as newly accepted models.

## Evidence and priority

The actual `_TEXT/ENGLISH.CDB` was read and SHA-256 checked against `cultural-architecture-index.json` (`767991db2dd69ffc182b80ea7f4a1a872935fbaa308f8778244a6b0e5fb3b4e4`). All six Architecture records were independently checked at their recorded offsets and byte lengths. Correctly paletted construction descriptors were inspected in the overview and enlarged PBS2/BRS2/MKS2/ARS2/MNS2/ACS2 pairs. `shared-construction-references.json` preserves palette, descriptor and source identities; original pixels remain reference-only.

The user's precedence is explicit: CDB lore overrides a visual material interpretation where they conflict. New construction mechanisms and unspecified details may be authored for OpenFA3D, but must not be represented as recovered lore or original geometry.

## Model and presentation contract

Outputs are under `docs/assets/reconstruction/scaffolds/culture-kits/` in the assembled workspace, published as editable sources under `sources/docs/assets/reconstruction/scaffolds/culture-kits/`, material maps under `sources/materials/scaffolds/culture-kits/` and finished media under `gallery/media/docs/assets/reconstruction/scaffolds/culture-kits/`. Existing models and their acceptance reports remain unchanged.

Every used family has standalone `families/<family>/stage-NN/model.blend` and `model.glb` for each stage 1..its actual source-derived maximum. Native sources preserve objects, meshes, materials, camera and placement/tier roots. Repeated meshes share geometry. `PlacementRoot` exports family/culture, footprint, construction stage and sequence extras. Tier roots export `stage_frame`; BottomSocket/TopSocket are separated by the nominal pitch. Single ground foundations are not repeated up the stack. Only complete lower tiers can support another tier. Crowns exist once at the top, not once per repeated tier.

The four user-required phases are erection, reveal, dismantling and complete. The target building is hidden during erection, inserted when the scaffold reaches its target height, and remains visible during reverse-order dismantling so its top is exposed first. Completion removes the scaffold. Each manifest contains ascending and exactly reversed stage lists for its real building definitions. The simulation/presenter owns timing and progress; the renderer must not infer phases or progress from these files. This requirement is not newly claimed legacy timing evidence.

New kits have perimeter supports and an open interior. Temporary crown nodes have `scaffold_role=temporary-crown`, `reveal_visible=false` and explicit names in each case's `hide_on_reveal`; remove them at reveal to clear the volume before inserting the building. All remaining supports stay on the perimeter until their reverse stage is removed. Existing RIS2 has its separately documented interior-shoring mask. These node classifications must survive model conversion if used by runtime presentation.

Pitch is derived from the recovered `capacity*4` pixel repeat and the family source width in a diagonal orthographic projection. It remains a provisional model-space choice, not recovered world height. Game integration must calibrate footprint, origin, target height and clearance against the real finished model, including overhangs. Do not infer the correct family from appearance, reuse one culture's model for all cultures, or treat source ceiling and construction duration as the same value. Original ownership-based family selection remains a separate evidence question.

The authored shells/frames intentionally provide stackable open volume and removable crowns. They are OpenFA3D interpretations informed by original silhouettes and cultural descriptions; they are not exact pixel-mask reconstructions of the original domes, interior forms or complete compositor.

## Validation workflow

Run the texture author, then `build_culture_scaffolds.py` in Blender 4.5 LTS through the assembled workspace. `--preview --families PBS2 BRS2 MKS2 ARS2 MNS2 ACS2` renders design previews separately; preview-only files must not substitute for completed family manifests. Run `verify_culture_scaffolds.py` for actual native/independent GLB inspection and imported renders, then `review_culture_scaffolds.py` for framed-view and pixel comparison reports. `test_culture_scaffold_contract.py` exercises ranges and the user-required reveal/dismantling contract for all 177 definitions.

Native/imported technical checks and comparison renders establish the export contract. They do not claim final game integration, timing correctness or exact legacy fidelity. Runtime integration and publication-ready colony captures remain game-repository work.

`integration.json` is the normalized runtime entry point. It includes all 177 original `global_type` keys, family selection, per-definition source ceiling and duration as separate values, ascending/reversed stage IDs, and all 281 GLB states including preserved RIS2. `glb` and `manifest` paths are relative to the canonical repository root and already include `sources/`; `workspace_glb`/`workspace_manifest` support assembled authoring workspaces. All referenced GLB hashes are checked when generating this index. The hide-on-reveal mask is normalized across new temporary crowns and existing RIS2 interior shoring.

The export pipeline additionally runs `finalize_culture_scaffold_normals.py` on MK/AC after generation and before final verification. A focused actual Blender diagnostic found that expressing the native curved sides and flat caps using explicit original split normals on smooth faces matches glTF's shading representation; it reduces native/imported mean RGB error without changing geometry, UVs, contour design or materials. The 65 affected native sources were actually reopened, their views rerendered, and their GLBs re-exported and reverified. Each affected manifest binds this finalizer as an additional source input.

The coverage reviewer distinguishes true geometry loss from stochastic edge antialiasing at 24 Cycles samples: isolated native/glTF float differences can flip a ray sample (about 11/255 alpha). Binary differences must remain within a one-pixel boundary band in both views; at most eight binary pixels and 64 alpha pixels may differ, and total absolute coverage error must stay below two fully covered pixels in a 512×512 image. Interior holes and displaced geometry are rejected, as exercised by focused regression tests. The RGB tolerance remains unchanged at 1.5/255 masked channel mean. Reports preserve the actual measured differences rather than claiming exact alpha equality.

## Verified result (2026-11-02)

All 18 new families and 264 distinct native sources/standalone GLBs passed actual Blender 4.5.15 checks. The 65 MK/AC states affected by the split-normal correction were exported, rerendered and independently verified again after the correction. All 616 front/rear/imported views passed framing, RGB and bounded coverage checks. Maximum masked RGB channel mean error is 0.2018/255. Maximum absolute alpha coverage difference is 1.604 fully covered pixel equivalents per 512×512 view, with at most five binary threshold pixels and 39 alpha pixels changed. These are isolated stochastic edge samples; reports retain the measured values rather than asserting exact equality. The subpixel-slot exception requires both alpha values near the threshold and no more than two ray samples of change; opaque interior holes remain rejected.

The six-culture overview and all eighteen maximum-stack renders were visually inspected, including the eleven-tier Mauna limit. The normalized integration index checked every GLB hash and all 177 original global-type keys, 19 used families and 281 total states including the preserved RIS2 refinement.
