# Destructor retrospective concept and GLB feedback

Programme openfa-assets #248. The accepted Terran source is preserved byte-for-byte. This completes a retrospective concept/render feedback step, not a new geometric remaster or final artistic acceptance.

One built-in ImageGen request used actual original PS03.VID rotation frames 0/15/30/45 as reference. The exact prompt and image identity are recorded in [the review](destructor-protocol-review.json). The generated concept is useful for broad wedge, paired aft engine structures, grey-violet alloy and red markings. Extra bow projections, small pods under the tabs and the merged aft shoulder treatment are interpretations and excluded from recovered geometry authority. No original pixels are published.

Fresh Blender 4.5.15 renders reopen the saved native source, then remove every source mesh/empty and independently import GLB under the same camera/light rig. Two corresponding front/rear quarter views check the same unchanged model. Mesh names and bounds match within 4.48e-8 scene units. Pixel metrics and raw render hashes are recorded; this is not a complete topology, UV, mount or client validation.

Visual feedback: the current model retains the principal wedge, red side strips, paired exhausts and outboard tabs. Its aft shoulders are flatter/narrower than the original; its red aft patches are smaller; its dorsal grille is raised rather than recessed. Hull finish and detail are simpler than the concept. These are recorded limits, not a claim that the concept and GLB are identical. The user's canonical Terran decision remains authoritative; no automatic replacement, new payload copy or wear retrofit follows from this review.

The interactive gallery includes the concept and actual imported views. There are no newly calibrated mounts, gameplay scale, state transitions or construction layers in this milestone. Other Terran protocol entries remain open.
