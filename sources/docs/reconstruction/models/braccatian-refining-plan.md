Follow-up to the completed Rigellian pass (#185 / #187). Work starts from
`cf2843c751be17d697d65e6b9e43136f9261354a` in an isolated assets worktree,
preserving the pending Rigellian studies and excluding concurrent scaffold work.

## Evidence and priorities

The complete Braccatian CDB entry (records 33–61) and cross-culture mentions
were read. `braccatian-lore-evidence.json` records exact offsets and hashes.
All 38 functional catalogue/name mappings were checked, with full building
narratives read. The broad architecture describes large, richly decorated
buildings with room for communal events; the actual building function qualifies
that guidance. Pithoi explicitly take the form of decorated storage jars.
TechMech is extensive and practical, Aesir's Hall explicitly lacks a ceremonial
role, and the Field Hospital is basic. Bronze Age cultural comparison does not
establish bronze as a universal construction material. High technology remains
part of the culture; arbitrary clan emblems, anatomical motifs and ritual
machinery must not be invented.

An initial correctly paletted comparison was inspected for every definition.
The 23 building catalogue films and all surface/ship/missile descriptors were
decoded privately. Building films present reveal/highlight phases, not spatial
turntables. Detailed per-model phase/heading/activity reviews remain required
before modelling and acceptance. Original pixels stay in ignored artifacts.
Sci-Tek films must be resolved through actual subject bindings and reviewed where
applicable; generic or other-culture diagrams do not override Braccatian sprites.

The baseline buildings are mostly generic silhouette placeholders. Significant
missing construction, colour divisions and equipment require more than surface
ornament. Existing BR11 launch motion and fixed-open BR12/BR19 shafts must retain
their proven hierarchy/clearances; new detail must follow their actual bodies.

## Completion contract

Every new study preserves prior source files and receives native Blender reopen,
independent GLB checks, meaningful construction/material/hierarchy tests and
rendered comparison. Prioritize visible missing parts and overall fidelity,
not microscopic scratches or mesh counts. New authored objects receive readable
material-appropriate wear. A model retained unchanged needs a focused reasoned
review and fresh validation, and is not counted as newly refined. Actual exported
clips need gallery WebM previews; yaw rigs do not imply idle animations.

Commit verified milestones and publish technical English issue comments with
SHA. Final audit must cover all 38 decisions, earlier model preservation and
gallery clip integrity. Notify the authorized game-integration chat with exact
committed paths. Final artistic acceptance, hidden construction and gameplay
calibration remain separate; do not edit the active game/scaffold checkout.
