Smaller distances to evaluated surfaces reflect raw skeletons before bevel modifiers or centreline curves inside their solid tubes. They are not evidence of different hardware. This allows intentional diagram LOD subsets without forcing every cosmetic bevel into the technical drawing.

Improved Sensors has11 additional authored front-panel tessellation lines, explicitly generated by build_sensors.py. The remaining168 chassis segments and every segment of its other three component groups match the physical skeleton.
