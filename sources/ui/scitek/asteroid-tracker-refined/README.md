They identify the right CRT interface, the square-framed fan pair, the **left upper data-bank module**, and a **separate countersunk threaded screw detail**. The screw's cabinet mounting point is not established by the inspected frames. Its staging coordinate is authored; it is hidden from solid geometry and the normal whole-device wireframe. The technical closeup isolates this auxiliary detail instead of inventing a visible mounting on the case. Tiny cylinder meridians are reduced for legibility; the helical thread and pointed tip remain explicit.

The isolated calibration detail is shown only during its closeup.444 frames are caption-selection windows; that count does not mean the small component is visibly red in every wide view.
