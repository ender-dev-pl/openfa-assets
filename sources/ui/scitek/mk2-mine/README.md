# Mk2 Mine — Sci-Tek #208, legacy index 00

Dedicated research-display mining assembly reconstructed from actual ST00.256, colour-preview phases and all three information-caption start/mid/end views. This is the Mk2 Mine upgrade presentation, not a replacement for an existing colony building model. Original source images remain private references; none are embedded as textures.

The tall alloy housing, olive pressure chamber with pale braces, long ribbed drive barrel, two lateral slurry ports, four independent multi-headed carbon cutting assemblies and two upper coolant sockets follow the inspected views. Material noise/bump, recessed grime and worn seams represent used mining equipment. Shape details, underside cutting-tip subdivisions and wire tessellation are authored interpretations; caption ownership is source-bound. The normal solid view is deliberately olive/grey, while selected technical components are red.

Technical display LOD omits the individual small carbon teeth and tiny collar fasteners; their dense lines otherwise merge into solid colour at the 224×224 information resolution. It retains all four drill envelopes/crowns. Solid geometry keeps all 16 teeth and the fasteners.

The two coolant sockets are placed on opposed sides of the upper casing. This is an authored topology interpretation of the colour turntable (one socket visible in the inspected exterior views) and the through-wire closeup (both sockets visible). The coolant study tilts the device to reproduce their diagonal screen separation. Exact original socket transforms were not recovered.
