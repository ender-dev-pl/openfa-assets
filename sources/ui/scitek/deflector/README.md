# Sci-Tek Deflector (research 08)

Reuses all 35 meshes of the existing PH10 Deflector without changing their vertices, faces, UVs, material slots, local transforms or wear. The original source remains unchanged. The presentation supplies the original 120x80 logical tile format, enlarged and disabled tiles, a 60-frame solid rotation, wireframe and three highlight variants, and the complete 423-frame information sequence at 15 fps.

Actual paletted PH10 static/preview and ST08 information frames were inspected. The source captions identify the energising nose cone (29..139), over-pressure relief valve (170..280), and auxiliary electronics (311..421). Only distal cone wire segments are selected in the first phase; the proximal taper remains green. The rear pin is the relief-valve highlight; side details, rear bracket and conduits form the authored electronics grouping. These classifications follow visible source highlights and remain approximate where the existing simplified construction differs from the original.

Approximately 80% visual correspondence is the current acceptance criterion. Existing source artistic gaps remain deferred: simplified flat rear bracket, boss geometry and authored rib counts/layout. No new operating mechanism or independent articulation is invented. Camera, pose, wire topology and zoom interpolation approximate observed original phases rather than recovered 3D data.

The information Blend has animated technical hue and camera. The information GLB uses unlit technical materials and named component geometry. Client playback must consume information-timeline.json: root/camera transforms, ortho_scale, highlight_component and caption_component independently. Use step hue changes; material colour and orthographic scale are not portable glTF animation channels. Native material flashes follow the observed red-activity clock independently from captions. Do not display hidden solid meshes in information mode.
