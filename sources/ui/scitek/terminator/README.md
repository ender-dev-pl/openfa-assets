# Sci-Tek Terminator research variant

Presentation derivative for ST30 / research30, reusing the existing supplemental openfa:terminator source at sources/docs/assets/reconstruction/ships/terran/terminator/model.blend. The initial Sci-Tek mapping missed this existing model; it is preserved in place. The derivative retains45 authored hull/wing/main-engine meshes, bind transforms, material assignments, UVs and all four packed wear images. Six generic forward projections are replaced in this presentation copy by source-labelled anterior devices; paired lateral manoeuvring modules are added. The canonical ship source is unchanged.

The actual ST30 colour tile/60-frame rotation and all three caption-anchor studies establish the anterior sensor/communications projections with central short rotary cannon, lateral manoeuvring pods and twin main exhausts. Exact hidden joint geometry, individual probe functions and original camera/mesh topology were not recovered. The new shapes/attachment depths are authored interpretations. Original legacy pixels are private references, not textures; the four reused texture images are previously authored wear maps.

Anterior assembly has two distinct stepped probe housings, fine antennae and a five-barrel short rotary head. Lateral pods sit at the forward outer wing corners. Main-thruster selection comprises existing exhausts/throats/cooling parts; hull engine housings remain green. New surfaces have procedural wear plus four explicit abrasion meshes; existing material wear is retained. GLB embeds four authored wear images. Blender procedural noise on new parts is not baked into textures.

Solid:60 frames/15fps, full negative-Z rotation on TerminatorResearchRotor, scale[1,1,1], loop closes at Blender61. Preserve all component bind transforms and use solid_preview_camera. Its scale is computed over every solid vertex at all60 poses with6 percent envelope margin; solid_framing records all projected bounds. Native/disabled144x160 and4x576x640 tiles contain the model in recovered ST30 box(4,40)..(122,113) without stretching.

Information:423 frames/15fps, chassis plus three selected groups. Anterior-device-assembly29..139, lateral-manoeuvring-thrusters170..280, twin-main-engine-thrusters311..421. A separate information GLB/Blend and information-timeline.json supply complete XYZ Root/camera/ortho/hue state. The initial Root orientation is the authored three-axis pose in whole_information_rotation_euler; replace the retained bind pose when applying timeline entries. Do not assume a shared yaw-only initial pose. Explicit hue and ortho scale are required because glTF alone does not portably animate them.

Technical display keeps full hull edges and adds an authored18x18 upper/lower retessellation on the existing bilinear wing planes. Engine castings use a reduced display cage; fine cooling/vent fins and tiny scuff geometry are omitted from display-wire LOD. This does not modify solid geometry, UVs or textures, and does not claim recovered legacy topology. Camera interpolation follows inspected anchors/order; original camera transforms were not recovered.

Bind scitek:terminator / openfa:presentation/profile/research/30. Paths and views are in gallery/media/ui/scitek/integration.json.
