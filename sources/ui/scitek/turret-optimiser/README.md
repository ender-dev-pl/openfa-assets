# Sci-Tek Turret Optimiser

Dedicated research presentation for ST31 / research31. The actual tile,60-frame colour rotation and all three exact caption-anchor studies establish an open frame between two shield plates, four longitudinal rails, a stepped energy-output fitting, a side manual-pulse rod and two internal flux capacitors. No suitable existing authored model was located. Original pixels are private evidence and are not model textures.

Stored-energy-output includes the front mounting/middle/neck/terminal sections. Manual-pulse-control includes the long bronze rod, rounded end and bearings. Flux-capacitor-system includes the two unequal capsule bodies, visible couplings and connecting yoke; the protective maroon cover stays chassis. The classified circuitry, hidden connections, plate depth and precise original topology were not recovered; these details are authored interpretations. The colour film demonstrates whole-assembly rotation rather than a pumping mechanism, so this asset does not invent such motion.

New metal has procedural wear, grime around contacts and16 explicit scuff meshes retained in portable PBR. Three closed curves form the rear concentric engraving. Small fasteners/scuffs are omitted from display-wire LOD. Blender procedural noise is not baked into GLB textures. Axial hardware has smooth side normals while retaining closed end faces.

Solid:60 frames/15fps on OptimiserRotor, scale[1,1,1], negative-Z rotation, loop closes at Blender61. Use solid_preview_camera. The camera is fitted to the recovered source occupancy while checking every solid vertex at all60 poses with minimum6 percent rotational envelope margin; solid_framing records all projected bounds. Native/disabled144x160 and registered4x576x640 tiles preserve box(41,41)..(109,123), position and alpha without stretching.

Information:423 frames/15fps, four wire groups. Stored-energy-output29..139, manual-pulse-control170..280, flux-capacitor-system311..421. Settled views show the stepped fitting, rod and paired capsules. A separate information GLB/Blend and information-timeline.json provide XYZ Root pose, camera, orthographic scale and component hue. The initial three-axis pose is in whole_information_rotation_euler; replace the retained bind pose when applying the timeline rather than adding it twice. Explicit hue/ortho scale are needed because glTF does not portably animate them. Framing/camera interpolation follows inspected source anchors but does not claim recovered original camera transforms.
