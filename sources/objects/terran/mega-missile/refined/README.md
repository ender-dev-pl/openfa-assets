# Shared Mega Missile

Authoritative openfa:mega for projectile gameplay, equipment catalogue and research13. Reuses the existing Terran revision2 model, with 56 original parts unchanged and one pressure casing refined below Z1.20. Eight earlier research additions are retained unchanged. Seven inferred lower covers/nozzle parts were removed so the four source-described thrust chambers remain visible in both the solid and technical presentation. The old reconstruction and research solids remain historical authoring inputs, not runtime copies.

The clearer actual ST13 lower-engine diagram informs the narrower neck. Dimensions are authored approximations, not recovered original 3D data. Threefold fins remain an existing interpretation. All 65 physical meshes are closed with positive volume; four scoped interior witnesses verify chamber/casing attachment. No new wear retrofit was applied. Existing finish, UVs, material slots and transforms of unchanged parts are retained.

MegaResearchRoot is unit scale, gameplay frame1 identity, nose+Z/front-Y. Disable its presentation rotation for projectile use. Runtime packages one shared coloured model.glb and the research information GLB/timeline; baked wire/highlight GLBs and category assemblies are authoring/gallery only. Client/GPU, flight axis, scale and mount calibration remain pending.
