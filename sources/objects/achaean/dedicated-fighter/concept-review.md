# Dedicated Fighter concept review

AC1C supplies 32 populated paletted headings. Reviewed all headings and enlarged 0/4/8/12/16/20/24/28. Upright thin parallel crescent plates open towards the long fore projection, opposite the yellow-marked shorter axial end. Grey rims, red root fields and warm slender body distinguish the fighter from the transporter's fuller body and reverse crescent orientation.

Two built-in ImageGen iterations used original paletted reference boards. Iteration 1 hid the second plate in a near side view. Corrective iteration 2 retained the metal/detail interpretation and exposed the paired geometry. It is accepted as an authoring concept, not recovered legacy or final user approval. Its hidden service structures, exact proportions and plausible manufacturing details remain interpretation. No extra equipment or glass domes are inferred.
