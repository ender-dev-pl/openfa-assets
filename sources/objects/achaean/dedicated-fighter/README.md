# Achaean Dedicated Fighter remaster

Canonical identity `openfa:ship/achaean/dedicated-fighter`, AC1C/global38/local1, issue [#236](https://github.com/ender-dev-pl/openfa-assets/issues/236). Use this current Blend/GLB for all gameplay/build/SciTek paths. The completed first editable source remains preserved in `sources/docs/assets/reconstruction/ships/achaean/dedicated-fighter/`; it is historical and excluded from the current selection and packaging.

All 32 correctly paletted legacy headings were reviewed. The paired thin upright silver/red crescents open towards the long forward projection, opposite the yellow aft detail. The slender warm axial body and lateral links retain their own proportions; this is not the transporter's geometry. End-on legacy views resolved central hull depth better than the concept: the first render was too flat and the final core was made fuller vertically. The independently imported final hull/crescent height ratio is approximately0.571, an authored diagnostic rather than a recovered physical measurement. No roof domes, invented weapons or operational animations are inferred. Four equipment slots remain uncalibrated.

Three built-in ImageGen iterations used the actual paletted board. Corrective feedback exposed both wing plates, then reduced excessive lateral spacing. The final concept has an inconsistent small inset; final concept acceptance remains unresolved for that inset, which is excluded from geometric authority. The candidate model follows the original headings directly rather than claiming a fully accepted concept. The selected concept and production brief are preserved; original generated images and original legacy pixels remain outside the repository. Original pixels are never model textures. Achaean Architecture record166 supplies curved-form context, not proof of vehicle domes, towers or equipment. Hidden service fittings, exact sections, materials and unseen surfaces are identified as interpretation.

The model has 35,000 triangles, 12 mesh batches, one material and two packed2048-square PBR maps. It intentionally shares the existing Achaean worn-metal atlas under `sources/materials/achaean/dedicated-ore-transporter/`, avoiding another texture cache. The builder reuses primitive/UV/material/studio helpers from the transporter generator but defines the fighter's hull, crescents and fittings independently; both generator and shared-helper hashes are bound in the reports. The transporter model, maps and helper files are unchanged. Material dirt, scratches, subtle plate dents and quiet broad surfaces retain the common palette without a dense grid.

Local forward-Y/up+Z; native maximum extent18. Proposed world size96 is an authored assumption matching the current generic client fallback, not recovered metres or a calibrated ship-heading conversion. Actual client scale/orientation, equipment and GPU performance require separate acceptance. No final user artistic approval is inferred from technical tests.

The cleaned saved Blend and independent GLB were opened/rendered in Blender4.5.15. Runtime checks cover closed nonzero core geometry, identical geometry/triangles, packed maps, eight empty forward-mouth/eight occupied-rim witnesses in both representations and zero calibrated sockets. Pixel comparison, framing and publication checks are recorded separately. Actual imported heading renders and the technical wireframe use that same geometry. Both galleries include current source/export downloads, views and the concept/model comparison. Reports are bound to current source/export hashes; final user and client review remain separate.

The coupled end/side review also reduced crescent arm flare and lateral spacing. The final overall width/length ratio is approximately0.522, guided by original end-on/side sprite spans30/59; projection/hidden geometry remain approximate, not recovered camera calibration. The source/body correction and final original-view comparisons are recorded as model feedback, not an automated fidelity score.

Final side-view comparison also restored the small non-emissive yellow bands on the forward axial section visible in AC1C headings4/20. These complement the short yellow aft terminal rather than copying the older concept's unmarked needle.
